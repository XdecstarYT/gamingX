SOCCERPRO'26

Full 11v11 arcade football. Kick off in seconds.

Keyboard + touch supported · Best on desktop

HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ Keyboard

  • Move — WASD or Arrow keys
  • Sprint — hold Shift
  • Pass / Tackle — X or K
  • Shoot / Slide — C or L (hold to power up)
  • Switch player — Z or J
  • Pause — P or Esc

📱 Touch

  • Move — virtual stick, left side of screen
  • PASS / SHOOT / SPRINT / SWITCH — buttons, right side
  • Hold SHOOT to charge power

⚽ The Game

  • 11v11, two halves, running match clock.
  • You control the highlighted player — the rest of your squad is run by AI.
  • Off the pitch means throw-ins, corners and goal kicks.
  • Keepers catch, punt and dive. Beat them with placed or powered shots.
  • Arcade rules: fast, physical, no fouls, no offside. Just score.
