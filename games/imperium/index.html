IMPERIUMWORLD CONQUEST

Pick a nation. Build an economy. Raise an army. Conquer the map.

HOW TO PLAY

🗺️ Provinces

The map is a web of provinces. Click one you own to manage it, click an adjacent one to consider attacking it.

💰 Economy

Every province generates gold, manpower and research each tick. Improvements and technology multiply your output.

⚔️ Combat

Send a share of a province's garrison to attack an adjacent province. Bigger, better-teched armies win — but losses are never free.

🔬 Technology

Spend research points to unlock stronger units and nationwide bonuses.

🤖 Rivals

AI nations build, research and attack on their own — aggressive, economic or balanced. They will come for you too.

🏆 Victory

Conquer every province, or simply outlast every rival nation. Lose your last province and it's game over.
