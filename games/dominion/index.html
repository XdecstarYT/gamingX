DOMINIONNATIONS AT WAR

Build a settlement. Pass your laws. Raise an army. Conquer the map.

HOW TO PLAY

🏗️ Build

Pick a building from the Build tab, then click an owned tile on the map to place it. Buildings take time and treasury to complete.

💰 Economy

Tax rates and funding levels (Economy tab) drive treasury, GDP, inflation and debt. Every lever has a trade-off.

🏛️ Politics

Approval, corruption and elections (Politics tab) determine whether you stay in power. Pass laws to shape your nation.

🔬 Research

Research labs and universities generate points. Spend them in the Tech tab to unlock new buildings and bonuses.

🌍 Diplomacy

Trade, ally with, or aid the AI nations sharing your world. Relations remember your past actions.

⚔️ War

Build a Military Base, train an army (Territory tab), then declare war (World tab) and attack adjacent enemy territory to conquer it. They can attack back.

⚡ Events

Random events will test your nation. Every choice has consequences — there is no single correct answer.
